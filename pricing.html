Pricing FAQ

Everything you need to know about how we structure our partnerships and billings.

Is ad spend included in the fee?

No, our fees cover our management and creative services. You pay the ad platforms directly for the media spend.

Are there long-term contracts?

We typically start with a 3-month initial term to allow for proper data gathering and scaling. After that, it moves to month-to-month.

Do you take a percentage of ad spend?

Our pricing is primarily flat-fee based to ensure our interests are aligned with your profitability, not just how much you spend.
