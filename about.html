About Us

About Naizo

Naizo ek Bihar-based digital agency hai jo schools, clinics, restaurants, coaching institutes aur local businesses ko online grow karne aur unke liye real customers lane ke liye bani hai.

Hum complicated jargon nahi, simple results pe focus karte hain: better websites, stronger social media presence, zyada phone calls, WhatsApp inquiries, appointments aur business growth.

Our Story

Hum Kyun Shuru Kiye?

Hamne Naizo tab start kiya jab hame laga ki chhote businesses ke paas do option hote hain: ya to khud sab handle karo (aur time waste karo), ya phir aisi agencies hire karo jo unki language hi nahi samajhti.

Hum khud tier-2 aur tier-3 cities se aate hain, isliye hame pata hai ki ek school, clinic, coaching institute ya local dukaan ke liye digital ka real matlab kya hai: zyada student admissions, zyada patient bookings, zyada inquiries aur zyada daily customers.
